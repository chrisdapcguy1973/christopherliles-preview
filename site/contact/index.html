What to include

  • Contract / consultingA scoped piece of work: network cleanup, a server or virtualization build, a migration.
  • Homelab or post questionSomething about the lab or a field note. Link the post and say what you have already tried.

Also mention your organization (for role and contract inquiries), the scope or environment, and your timeline. Leave out passwords and internal IPs.
